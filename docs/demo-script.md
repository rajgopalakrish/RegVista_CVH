# RegVista — Demo script (draft)

Target: under 3 minutes, live product, no localhost.

1. **Hook (10s)** — "You've heard of a company. Do you know its regulatory
   world? RegVista tells you."
2. **Enter a company (15s)** — Type a real, recognizable company name into
   RegVista. Click "Research."
3. **Watch it work (30s)** — Show the research run go from pending → running
   → done in real time (Convex reactivity, no page refresh). Narrate:
   Firecrawl is pulling from regulator/primary sources, OpenAI is turning
   that into structured findings.
4. **Explore the landscape (60s)** — Walk through the resulting view:
   jurisdictions, regulators, regulatory areas, and 2-3 concrete findings —
   for each, show the relevance score, the "why this matters" line, and
   click through to an evidence source.
5. **Email the briefing (20s)** — Click "Email me this briefing," show the
   AgentMail-sent email landing in an inbox.
6. **Close (15s)** — Restate the company-centric pitch and the stack: Convex
   (backend + reactivity), Firecrawl (retrieval), OpenAI (structuring),
   AgentMail (delivery). Show the live URL.

_To be refined once the MVP is built and rehearsed against a real company._
